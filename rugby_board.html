Rugby League Fair Value Board

Fair margins come from a two-stage OLS per league: stage 1 predicts both scores from home/away split averages plus a decayed net-form term; stage 2 calibrates the raw margin against walk-forward out-of-sample results. Raw margin is the number to hold against a spread; fair MOV is the shrunk single-game prediction, and the shrinkage factor is the model's earned conviction (NRL ~31%, SL ~75%). Edge = raw margin − market-implied margin. Win probabilities are the fair MOV read through the model's own out-of-sample error. Totals carry no out-of-sample signal in either league and are shown for reference only. Everything here is paper until the CLV ledger proves the edges real.

Ledger is paper on a risk/win $100 basis: each bet is sized to win $100 at the best price on offer when it was logged, so the stake is 100/(price−1) — $114.94 at 1.87 (−115). A win returns $100, a loss costs the stake, and ROI is P/L over the total risked.
